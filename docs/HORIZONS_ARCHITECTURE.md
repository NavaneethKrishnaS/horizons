# HORIZONS Architecture

## Project Vision

HORIZONS is a premium Kerala travel platform focused on curated travel experiences rather than online bookings.

Unlike traditional hotel booking websites, HORIZONS helps travellers discover luxury stays, houseboats, curated experiences and travel packages before connecting them directly with the HORIZONS team to check availability and plan their journey.

The website serves as a luxury digital concierge rather than an online booking engine.

---

# Primary Goals

- Showcase Kerala's finest luxury stays and houseboats.

- Inspire travellers through premium editorial photography.

- Generate qualified enquiries through WhatsApp, Phone and Email.

- Offer personalised travel planning instead of automated booking.

- Build trust through elegant design and curated recommendations.

---

# Business Model

HORIZONS does not process payments online.

Instead:

Visitor

↓

Browse destinations

↓

Browse hotels / houseboats / packages

↓

Check Availability

↓

Contact HORIZONS

↓

Receive personalised quotation

↓

Trip planning

↓

Booking confirmation

# Core Principles

- Luxury first.

- Minimal design.

- Editorial photography.

- Fast navigation.

- Mobile-first.

- Personal travel consultation.

- No unnecessary forms.

- Human interaction over automation.

# Primary Navigation

The main navigation should remain minimal and editorial.

Desktop Navigation

- Destinations

- Houseboats

- Stays

- Packages

- Journal

Right CTA

Check Availability

Experiences are intentionally not part of the primary navigation.

Experiences are discovered naturally inside destinations, stays and packages.

This keeps the navigation focused on the user's primary intent.

---

Navigation Philosophy

Users usually arrive looking for:

- Houseboats

- Hotels

- Kerala Packages

They rarely search for experiences directly.

Experiences should enrich destinations and packages rather than compete with them in the navigation.

---

Navigation Goals

- Simple

- Minimal

- Luxury

- Easy to scan

- Maximum five navigation items
